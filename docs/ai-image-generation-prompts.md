# 🌺 BOTANICAL ELEMENT PROMPTS

## 1. Waratah Bloom (The "Heart")
**Context:** Central hero element or celebration icon.
> **Prompt:** A magnificent single Waratah (Telopea speciosissima) flower head in full bloom. Viewed from a slightly high angle. Deep crimson red petals (`#C45C4B`) with intricate serrated edges, glowing against the dark. The center cone is detailed and textured. The stem is thick and woody. Victorian botanical illustration style, highly detailed. Isolated on black.

## 2. Golden Wattle Spray (The "Hanging Anchor")
**Context:** Top-right corner decoration, draping down.
> **Prompt:** A cascading branch of Golden Wattle (Acacia pycnantha) hanging downwards from the top right. Large, fluffy spherical flower heads in vibrant golden yellow (`#D4A84B`) contrasting with matte green phyllodes (leaves). The composition should feel like it is draping gravity-defyingly. Soft lantern lighting hitting the yellow puffballs. Etched details. Isolated on black.

## 3. Banksia Cone (The "Grounding Anchor")
**Context:** Bottom-left heavy element.
> **Prompt:** A dried, woody Banksia seed pod cone sitting upright on a surface. Textured, gnarled surface with open 'mouths' (follicles). Warm amber and burnt orange tones (`#D4885C`). Heavy shadows at the base to ground it. Very high textural detail, feeling rough to the touch. Scientific study style. Isolated on black.

## 4. Ghost Gum Leaves (The "Texture")
**Context:** Background atmosphere and dividers.
> **Prompt:** A cluster of three elegant Eucalyptus leaves floating. The leaves are long, sickle-shaped, and smooth. Color is a pale, ghostly sage green (`#7A9E82`) with subtle veins. Soft, diffused lighting making them look almost translucent. Delicate watercolor and ink style. Isolated on black.

**Context:** Laboratory mode primary icon.

The needle is steel blue. Glass face reflects a singular candlelight source. Utilitarian but beautiful manufacturing. Macro view. Isolated on black.

## 6. Compass Rose (The "Map")
**Context:** Navigation headers, orientation.
> **Prompt:** A stylized 16-point compass rose illustration. Drawn in gold ink. Geometric precision mixed with ornate Victorian flourishes. No background paper texture, just the gold lines. Clean, symmetrical, elegant. Design suitable for a map legend. Isolated on black.

## 7. Brass Key (The "Access")
**Context:** Authentication and security.
> **Prompt:** A single ornate Victorian skeleton key made of tarnished brass. The bow (handle) has an intricate scrollwork design. The shaft is sturdy. The bit (end) is complex. Lighting catches the metallic edges. Heavy distinct shadow. Symbol of unlocking secrets. Isolated on black.
